# 📊 Superstore Sales Analytics

A complete end-to-end Data Analytics project that analyzes retail sales data using **Python** and **Power BI**. The project focuses on discovering business insights through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA) and interactive dashboard development.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Superstore Sales dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, product trends, customer segments and regional performance.

# 📈 Exploratory Data Analysis

The following business questions were explored:

- What is the total sales generated?
- What is the total profit earned?
- How many orders were placed?
- What is the average order value?
- How have sales changed over time?
- Which product categories generate the highest sales?
- Which regions generate the highest sales?
- Which products perform the best?
- Which products perform the worst?
- How does discount affect profit?

# 🔍 Key Findings

- The business generated over **$2.30M** in total sales.
- Total profit exceeded **$286K**.
- Technology was the highest-performing product category.
- The West region contributed the highest sales.
- Sales showed a positive growth trend over the years.
- High discounts generally reduced profitability.
- A small number of products contributed significantly to total sales.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Continue investing in the Technology category.
- Optimize discount strategies to protect profit margins.
- Improve marketing efforts in lower-performing regions.
- Maintain inventory for top-selling products.
- Review low-performing products for optimization or replacement.
- Monitor business performance regularly using interactive dashboards.
